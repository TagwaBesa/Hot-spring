# Hot-Spring Oils Company Limited | One-page website

A responsive one-page corporate website built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It does not use Laravel or a front-end framework.

## Preview

1. Extract the ZIP archive.
2. Open `hotspring-onepage/index.html` in a modern browser.
3. Keep an internet connection active to load the hosted Unsplash photography and Google Fonts.

## What's included

- One-page navigation with links to sections on this page only.
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile.
- Scroll reveal and subtle image animations, with reduced-motion support.
- Expandable business/service details and expandable industry descriptions.
- Company profile details covering bulk petroleum products, lubricants, chemicals, fuel transportation, depot/storage solutions, fuel station design/development, mining, mineral processing and trading, general supply, stationery and office supplies.
- Mission and business outlook wording based on the supplied company profile.
- Contact details and an enquiry form that prepares an email to `info@hotspingoils.com`.

## Before publishing

- Replace the provisional graphic logo with the official company logo if available.
- Confirm all listed products, services and capabilities before publication.
- Review the stock photography and replace it with approved company photographs where possible. Remote images and fonts require an internet connection.
- The enquiry form opens the visitor's email application. It does not send/store form submissions itself. Connect a server-side form handler or trusted form service if direct submissions are required.
- Publish to the chosen hosting provider and configure the domain and HTTPS.

## Files

- `index.html`: all page content and sections
- `assets/css/styles.css`: layout, responsive design and animations
- `assets/js/main.js`: mobile navigation, scroll reveal, expandable details and enquiry form behaviour
